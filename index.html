FAQ

Article freshness QA constraints are explicit.

Does it crawl article URLs?

No. It produces a launch QA brief from pasted Article markup and notes only.

Does it verify Google News eligibility?

No. This is technical SEO and launch QA guidance, not eligibility, ranking, indexing, or editorial advice.

Does it call Search Console or Rich Results Test?

No. This MVP is browser-local and does not call Search Console, Rich Results Test, CMS platforms, newsroom APIs, or external APIs.

Does it validate paywall implementation?

No. It only flags paywall markup handoff gaps from pasted markup and notes.
