Checklist

Prelaunch article structured data QA

Use this with pasted samples, visible page notes, editorial notes, and CMS template notes. It is not a crawler, Google News eligibility checker, or Rich Results Test replacement.

1. Confirm the article entity

Use `Article`, `NewsArticle`, or `BlogPosting` for article pages. Do not mark up listing pages, tag pages, or unrelated landing pages as one article.

2. Match the visible headline

Compare schema `headline` with the visible H1/title. Flag stale migration titles, truncated template headlines, and syndicated rewrites.

3. Review published and updated dates

Check `datePublished` and `dateModified` against visible publish/update notes. Heavy revisions need a deliberate freshness decision.

4. Check author identity fields

Each visible author should map to an author object. Prefer a clear `Person` or `Organization` type plus name and a valid `url` or `sameAs` handoff.

5. Inspect article images

Confirm the Article image field maps to the intended hero or representative image, not a fallback, placeholder, default CMS image, or unrelated thumbnail.

6. Resolve canonical and syndication notes

Compare `mainEntityOfPage`, `url`, `@id`, visible canonical notes, old URLs, syndicated source notes, and migration ownership.

7. Flag gated-content markup gaps

If notes mention paid, member-only, subscriber-only, or registration-gated sections, confirm `isAccessibleForFree` and `hasPart` decisions are owned.

8. Assign cleanup owners

Name the editorial, frontend, CMS template, syndication, paywall, and SEO owners before the article or migration goes live.
